
# Metta People – Project Overview

## Introduction

Metta People is a multi-sided marketplace platform that connects customers with different types of service providers.

The platform supports three main provider types:

- **Facilitators** – Offering 1:1 Sessions, Workshops, and Programs.
- **Merchants** – Offering Physical Products.
- **Hosts** – Offering Accommodation.

The project includes multiple workflows covering user registration, provider onboarding, listing creation, search, booking, payments, order fulfilment, service completion, and reviews.

## My Role

**Role:** Manual QA Tester

My responsibility was to understand the application requirements, validate functional workflows, identify potential issues, prepare test documentation, and communicate QA observations to the project team.

I worked on both Provider-side and Customer-side workflows to understand and validate the complete platform experience.

## Testing Approach

I followed a structured QA approach:

1. Understand the requirements and expected functionality.
2. Analyse Provider and Customer workflows.
3. Identify test scenarios and edge cases.
4. Design positive, negative, and UI/UX test cases.
5. Execute manual test scenarios.
6. Compare actual results with expected results.
7. Document observations and potential issues.
8. Communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders.

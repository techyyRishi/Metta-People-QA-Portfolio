# Metta People – Provider & Customer End-to-End Journeys

## Project Overview

This document provides a visual, step-by-step overview of the Provider and Customer journeys within the Metta People platform.

The presentation was prepared to explain the platform's major workflows, from account creation and provider onboarding to listing publication, customer booking, completion, and reviews.

It covers three major listing types:

- Facilitator – 1:1 Session
- Merchant – Physical Product
- Host – Accommodation

## Part 1: Provider Journey

The Provider Journey documents the process of becoming a provider and creating a published listing.

### 1. Account Creation & Email Verification
- Provider account registration
- Email verification

### 2. Become a Provider
- Accessing the provider onboarding journey
- Selecting a provider type
- Completing provider information
- Submitting details for review

### 3. Provider Approval
- Admin verification of submitted provider details
- Approval before continuing provider setup

### 4. Stripe & Payout Setup
- Entering payout information
- Starting Stripe onboarding
- Account security and identity verification
- Adding business and bank details
- Completing customer-facing information
- Finishing Stripe setup
- Confirming payout setup

### 5. Listing Creation

The presentation covers listing creation for three provider types:

**Facilitator**
- Creating a 1:1 Session listing
- Adding service details and media
- Configuring availability
- Adding location and pricing
- Reviewing and publishing the listing

**Merchant**
- Creating a Physical Product listing
- Completing product information and listing setup
- Publishing the product listing

**Host**
- Creating an Accommodation listing
- Adding property details, location, and pricing
- Reviewing and publishing the accommodation listing

## Part 2: Customer Journey

The Customer Journey explains the customer experience from signup through booking, completion, and review.

### 1. Account & Discovery
- Customer signup
- Email verification
- Exploring the platform
- Searching for listings
- Opening listing details

### 2. 1:1 Session Booking
- Searching for a session
- Submitting a booking request
- Provider accepts or declines
- Service completion
- Review period

### 3. Physical Product Booking
- Searching for a product
- Selecting and purchasing a product
- Order processing
- Provider acceptance or decline
- Shipment and order receipt
- Review period

### 4. Accommodation Booking
- Searching for accommodation
- Selecting a stay
- Submitting a booking request
- Provider accepts or declines
- Stay completion
- Review period

### 5. Completion & Reviews

The presentation illustrates the common final stage across the three customer journeys:

- Booking or order fulfilment
- Completion confirmation
- Review period
- Customer and Provider review opportunities
